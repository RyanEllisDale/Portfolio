Modular Architecture Unity

Modular architecture is a small Unity package designed to provide structured re-usable data-driven pieces of code that can be used and expanded upon in your own Unity projects. Unity's codebase is inherently quite tightly coupled with the reign of Mono Behaviour and whilst it works and the workflow is very simple it has shortcomings.

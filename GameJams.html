C.A.P Jam (2025)

Teesside University & Itch.io 2025

Public Speaking Hosting Web-Dev Planning Budgeting Networking

View The Website

Theme: Invention

Continuing on from the year prior the games societies held another CAP Jam. This time 'round I was less directly involved in order to give the new Games' Creation lead some room to learn event-management and networking with industry volunteers. I was responsible again for the theme, website-development, and server-upkeep. Additionally, I developed a few new assets for this year built from the base designs of last year.

My Co-Hosts this time were Harry Poobalasingham, Nicole Double, and Emily Harman.

C.A.P Jam (2024)

Teesside University & Itch.io 2024

Advertising Graphic Design Planning Event Organisation Networking

View The Website

Theme: Desperation!

As an event for my Programming Society I joined with the sister Games Creation and Games Art societies to create the CAP Jan - the annual combined games society Jam. In this first year I was responsible for the advertisement, public speaking, server management, and Website Development. Moreover, I suggested and vouched for the theme that was ultimately chosen for the jam: Desperation!


This jam was very light-hearted in tone, being smaller than that of the following year due to leadership and venue changes. As a result of this tone a lot of the submissions are humour-focused, a result I greatly enjoy.

My Co-Hosts for this first CAP Jam were Harry Poobalasingham, and Sara Rushmer.

STC Jam (2023)

South Tyneside College 2023

Teaching Networking Public Speaking Presentations Judging / Voting

Theme: Arcade

After my time at South Tyneisde College I was invited back to hold a game jam for the student, the theme I decided on was "Arcade", they were to either develop a game project or a 3D environment. This was the first ever game-jam that I helped host and I was responsible for teaching the students what a game jam was, how to go about working quickly, and how to present their work. At the end of the given 7 hours of development me and my co-host Jack Warren judged and determined the winners amongst ourselves.
